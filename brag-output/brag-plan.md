# 1Poker — brag plan

**What:** Private, phone-first poker rooms for friends. Create a room, share a 5-letter code, play.
**For:** Friend groups who want a home game at 2 AM without a casino app, chips or an account.
**Sets it apart:** One room plays everything — Hold'em, Omaha 4/5/6, Irish, Pineapple, Short Deck, Teen Patti — with dealer's choice, plus stickers, sound stickers and voice notes at the table.
**Visual hook:** A 5-letter room code slamming onto a black table like dealt cards.
**Tone:** Cinematic (trailer-scale, big Bebas type, gold light-sweep wipes), late-night card-table: near-black, felt-green glow, gold.
**Format:** 1920×1080, 30fps, 12s (user-set). No voiceover.

**Identity (from `src/client/styles/global.css`):** bg `#05060b`, gold `#f7cb6f → #d8992d`, felt `#17905f / #0c5e3f / #05301f`, Bebas Neue display + Inter.

**Source footage:** Real app, captured by driving three phones (iPhone 13 viewport) through the repo's own e2e flow. Player names swapped to fictional "Kabir / Zoya / Rhea". The face-snap shot (fake camera feed) is excluded.
